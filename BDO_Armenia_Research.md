# BDO Armenia — Research Summary

## Overview

BDO Armenia is the Armenian member firm of BDO International Limited, one of
the world's largest networks of public accounting, tax, and advisory firms.
It is organized as a closed joint-stock company (CJSC) and provides
professional services to businesses operating in Armenia.

## Global Network Context

- **BDO International** was founded in 1963 and today operates in roughly
  164 countries and territories through more than 870 offices.
- For the financial year ending 30 September 2025, BDO reported global
  network revenue of **US$11 billion**, a 4% year-on-year increase (5-year
  CAGR of 8%). Including alliance-firm revenue, combined revenue reached
  **US$16 billion**.
- Globally, BDO employs over 94,900 people. Audit and Assurance remains the
  network's largest service line, with Tax (+7%) and Advisory (+4%) growing
  fastest in 2025.
- Each BDO member firm, including BDO Armenia, is a separate legal entity
  operating under the shared BDO brand and methodology rather than a single
  multinational corporation.

## BDO in Armenia

- BDO's presence in Armenia was established in **2010**, when its local
  member firm(s) joined the international network.
- The Armenian practice is headquartered in **Yerevan** (Davit Anhaghti St.,
  Building 23/6, 5th floor) and, per local directory listings, employs
  roughly **50–99 staff**.
- The firm positions itself around BDO's global "People helping people"
  philosophy and emphasizes building long-term client relationships.

## Services Offered

BDO Armenia provides five principal service lines, consistent with the
global BDO service model:

1. **Audit & Assurance** — statutory and voluntary financial statement
   audits under Armenian national standards as well as International
   Standards on Auditing (ISA), IFRS, and US GAAP; internal audit and other
   audit-related services.
2. **Tax Consultancy** — tax planning and structuring, optimization of
   taxation for international transactions, tax due diligence, and support
   in disputes with Armenian tax authorities.
3. **Business Services & Outsourcing** — accounting, bookkeeping, payroll,
   and related back-office support.
4. **Legal Advisory** — corporate and commercial legal support alongside
   audit/tax engagements.
5. **Advisory** — management consulting, risk advisory, and transaction
   support (e.g., due diligence, valuations).

## Notes on Sources

Public information about BDO Armenia's exact founding/incorporation history
is inconsistent across secondary sources (some cite 1996 for a predecessor
entity, others cite 2010 as the year it joined the BDO network). The 2010
date is the one BDO's own website consistently uses for "BDO in Armenia,"
so it is treated here as authoritative for the network affiliation date,
while the earlier date may reflect a locally founded predecessor firm.
